## Notes & risks

- **Negatives matter**: `none` images are kept to suppress false alarms (clouds, fog, sunsets, lights).
- **Small objects**: any sizeable `small` bucket justifies the high-resolution (832px) experiment for recall on distant fire/smoke.
- **Class asymmetry**: report class-wise AP so strong smoke performance cannot mask weak fire performance (or vice-versa).
- **Leakage**: D-Fire frames can come from the same source video; keep the official split and never move files between splits.
